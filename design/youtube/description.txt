Ein Yaakov with Rabbi Chaim Fogelman: the stories, lessons and wisdom of the Gemara, one daf at a time.

Ein Yaakov collects the aggadah of the Talmud, the stories of Chazal and their teachings on emunah, middos and how to live. Each week Rabbi Fogelman learns through the next section, bringing it to life with Rashi, the Rebbe and stories from the chassidic world.

The shiur is given Tuesday nights at 8pm at the Itchkes Shtiebel. New shiurim are posted every week.

Now learning: Masechta Kesuvos. The full library, including Baitza and Chagigah, is at einyaakovshiur.com, where you can search every shiur by masechta, daf or topic.

Also on Spotify, Apple Podcasts, Amazon Music and iHeart: search "Ein Yaakov with Rabbi Chaim Fogelman."
